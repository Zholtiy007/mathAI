How differentiation works

  • Foundation: smaller numbers, guided steps, and direct hints.
  • Standard: typical curriculum practice with fewer prompts.
  • Advanced: more complex equations or a contextual challenge.

The level changes the task, scaffolding, and explanation—not just the label.
